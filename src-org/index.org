I am a software engineer. I currently run [[https://loopless.xyz][Loopless]], my own
consulting / engineering vehicle.

Previously :: software engineer at [[https://o1labs.org][O(1) Labs]] (2022–2024) on
the Mina Protocol, [[https://nomadic-labs.com][Nomadic Labs]] (2021–2022) on the Tezos consensus
layer, and [[https://www.tweag.io/][Tweag I/O]] (2019–2021). Before that, eleven years as a
researcher at [[http://www-list.cea.fr/en/][CEA / LSL]] (2008–2019), working on formal program
analyses for source and binary code, and as lead developer of the
[[https://binsec.github.io][BINSEC]] binary analysis platform.

PhD in computer science from Université Paris 6 Pierre & Marie Curie
(2003–2006) — /Tableaux and Deduction Modulo/.

I am most at home in functional programming (OCaml, Lisp, Rust), formal
methods, program analysis, automated theorem proving, and the
boundaries where these meet practical software engineering.

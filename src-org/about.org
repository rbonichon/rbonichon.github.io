Before that, I spent 11 years as a researcher at [[http://www-list.cea.fr/en/][@@html:<abbr>CEA</abbr>@@ /
@@html:<abbr>LSL</abbr>@@]], working on formal program analyses for source and

I am most at home in functional programming (OCaml, Lisp, Rust), formal
methods, program analysis, automated theorem proving, and the
boundaries where these meet practical software engineering.

I am a software engineer. I currently run [[https://loopless.xyz][Loopless]], my own consulting /
engineering vehicle. I am working for Repyh Labs on the [[https://delta.network/en/][delta network]].

Previously, I also was a software engineer at 

- [[https://o1labs.org][O(1) Labs]] on the Mina Protocol;
- [[https://nomadic-labs.com][Nomadic Labs]] on the Tezos consensus layer; and
- [[https://www.tweag.io/][Tweag I/O]].

Before that, I spent 11 years as a researcher at [[http://www-list.cea.fr/en/][@@html:<abbr>CEA</abbr>@@ /
@@html:<abbr>LSL</abbr>@@]], working on formal program analyses for source and
binary code, and as lead developer of the [[https://binsec.github.io][@@html:<abbr>BINSEC</abbr>@@]] binary
analysis platform.

I am most at home in functional programming (OCaml, Lisp, Rust), formal methods,
program analysis, automated theorem proving, and the boundaries where these meet
practical software engineering. These last few years, I've also enjoyed messing
with distributed systems as they appear on blockchain protocols, and
zero-knowledge cryptography.
